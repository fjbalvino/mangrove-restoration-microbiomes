# Nitrogen depth: manuscript update

## Results

Depth was examined secondarily through paired 20/5 and 40/5 cm changes within profiles. Neither set of changes showed a supported difference among restoration stages in the primary analysis (all Holm-adjusted P = 1.000). This tests whether vertical patterns differ among stages; it does not test whether an overall depth effect exists. Individual profiles and within-stage distributions are shown in the accompanying depth analysis.

A nitrogen-focused depth analysis then compared 20 and 40 cm with 5 cm within the same 17 profiles. Among 49 eligible KOs, one decreased at 20 cm and 12 at 40 cm after Holm correction across 98 depth tests. Ten of the 40 cm contrasts retained support across all five quantification variants. These tests describe paired vertical changes across profiles, separately from whether those changes differ among restoration stages.

When the 98 depth tests were corrected jointly with the preceding 1,311 stage and stage-by-depth-change tests, three 40 cm contrasts remained supported in the primary analysis. Two retained support across all five quantification variants: nirS (K15864) and ferredoxin-dependent glutamate synthase (gltS, K00284). Both were lower at 40 cm in all 17 profiles. Median 40/5 cm ratios were 0.32 for nirS (pointwise 95.1% interval 0.095–0.468) and 0.56 for gltS (0.439–0.759), corresponding to median reductions of 68% and 44% (both primary Holm-adjusted P = 0.0215 across 1,409 tests). The gltS estimate retained provisional fractional assignments. These contrasts concern annotated genetic representation and do not establish changes in nitrogen-processing rates (Supplementary Table 16).

## Methods

For the exploratory nitrogen depth analysis, we retained the 49 eligible nitrogen KOs from the expanded panel and compared 20 and 40 cm with 5 cm within each profile. Exact two-sided sign tests assessed the direction of paired log₂ changes across the 17 profiles. We report median ratios and pointwise 95.1% order-statistic intervals under a common-median model; these intervals are not simultaneous, and heterogeneity among profiles limits their interpretation. Holm correction covered the 98 nitrogen depth tests. We also corrected them jointly with the preceding 1,311 tests, giving a combined family of 1,409. This does not cover every analysis explored in the study. We repeated the comparisons with all five quantification variants and retained the same eligible KO set. Within-stage depth summaries were descriptive because each stage contained only three to five profiles. Full results and sensitivity checks accompany Supplementary Table 16.

Discussion was not edited in this update.
